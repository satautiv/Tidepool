Privacy

Tidepool saves your progress and settings only on your device. It does not ask for an account and does not collect personal information.

On game portals and in the Android app, ads are provided by the platform (for example the portal's own ad service or Google AdMob), which may use device identifiers to show and measure ads under its own privacy policy. Where required you are asked for consent first.

Anonymous gameplay statistics (such as scores and session length) may be collected to improve the game. They contain no personal information.

This page will be updated before the public release.
